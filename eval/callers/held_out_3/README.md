Third held-out set: twelve callers (eight in English, four in Canadian French) written before the learned reader was
built, committed on their own, and run once at the end for the final number. Nothing in the reader, its training
corpus or its lexicons was tuned on them.

How the scripts are written: each caller answers the questions the line's scripts ask, in order. A fact the caller
states plainly in the opening is not asked again, except the date, time and rooms, which the line always asks. An
off-script question or a correction takes one turn, and the line then asks the pending question again.
Hitting an animal does not by itself answer whether another vehicle or person was involved, so the line still asks.
